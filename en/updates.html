Updates

What's new?

What's been added to Kanade, newest first. Announcements are also posted on the support server.

  1. 30 October 2026

    Card album: /collection

    • /collection is now a card album you flip through page by page: 6 of your actual cards on every page.
    • Pick a card to open it big; if you like it, show it to the server with Share.
    • Filter by rarity, or switch to the text list with ☰ List.
    • Card images open about 4 times faster.
    • Fixed some listens not being saved late at night.
  2. 29 October 2026

    New website

    • The introduction, features, every command, the guide and frequently asked questions now live on this site, in Turkish and English.
  3. 24 October 2026

    Animated /random cards

    • Rare and higher songs are now animated: a glowing frame or stars in the tier colour.
  4. 23 October 2026

    New level-up and /random cards

    • The level-up celebration and the /random card moved to the same new design as the profile card.
  5. 22 October 2026

    New profile card

    • Showcase: display your 3 favourite cards on your profile with /card showcase.
    • An XP bar, card counts by tier and a sharper image.
    • Music taste: your 3 most-played artists can appear on your profile; off by default, turn it on with /privacy music-taste.
    • Fewer outages: the bot now comes back on its own even after a long internet outage.
    • Fairer boxes on servers where only a few people listen: the server pool's share depends on how many people add songs to it.
  6. 20 October 2026

    Your own music in boxes

    • About a quarter of box cards now come from songs you played and don't own yet.
    • Players with tracking off see what that means in /start and /rates.
  7. 17–19 October 2026

    Kanade emojis

    • The bot's own emojis for Vinyl, Shards, boxes, tiers and more.
    • Fixed a bug that briefly stopped slash commands from responding.
  8. 16 October 2026

    Big update: shorter commands and dismantling

    • One-word commands: /box, /rates, /collection (with member, rarity and page options), /sets, /profile, /bio.
    • /card dismantle: turn a card you don't want into Shards.
    • More varied boxes: artists from your last boxes show up less often for a while; the odds didn't change.
    • A Guide in /help with "How to play" and a glossary; one-time tips for new players.
    • Pressing the /start buttons twice no longer opens a second box; if the bot can't post in a channel it now shows you a private note.
  9. 10–12 October 2026

    Closed beta begins

    • /leaderboard: your server's weekly and all-time rankings.
    • /privacy: see your data, turn tracking off or on, delete all your data. The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service were published.
    • A tip on how to show your Spotify status if it's hidden, a welcome message when the bot joins a server, and a categorised /help.
  10. 9–10 October 2026

    The foundations

    • Earning Vinyl by listening, music boxes, four tiers and edition numbers.
    • Shards and crafting, album sets, /daily rewards and quests.
    • Turkish and English; slash commands and the k! prefix.
