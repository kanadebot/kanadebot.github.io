How it works

A collection in three steps

Type /start in your server and press Join. Your first box is a gift.

  1. Listen on Spotify

    With your Spotify status showing on Discord, listen to a song for 30 seconds and earn Vinyl. The songs you play join your server's pool.

  2. Open a box

    Open a music box for 100 Vinyl. It holds cards of songs played on your server, and the odds are always public.

  3. Show your collection

    Put your favourite cards in your profile showcase, complete album sets and climb the leaderboard.

Privacy

Only what you already show

  • It doesn't connect to your Spotify account. No login, password or permission.
  • It only reads the "listening" status on Discord: song, artist, album.
  • Counted once you join with /start. Songs played by members who don't join enter the pool anonymously.
  • Delete any time: with /privacy you can view your data, turn tracking off or delete everything.
