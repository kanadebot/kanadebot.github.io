Cards and tiers

Every song is a card

Each card from a box is a song: cover, title, artist and album. A card's tier is set the moment it's minted and never changes.

  • Edition no.: which copy of that song you own. #1 is the first edition; the first 10 are marked "Early Edition". Numbers are never reused.
  • Discoverer: the player who first played the song on your server appears on its card.
  • /card show shares your card as an image in chat, /card info gives its edition, tier and set.
Tiers

Four tiers, public odds

Every box gives a Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary card. The odds and your personal counters are always listed in /rates.

Pity (guarantee): bad luck doesn't last forever. An Epic or better is certain within 10 boxes in a row, a Legendary within 45.

A new player's first box is at least Rare, and every 7th day of your /daily streak brings an Epic+ guaranteed box.

Sets and showcase

Complete albums, show off your best

Every album with at least 3 songs in the catalog is a set. Collect them all for 30 Vinyl per song and a permanent badge; track your progress in /sets. If a set grows later you can complete it again with the new songs.

The showcase on your profile card has 3 slots: put your favourite cards there with /card showcase. Slots you leave empty fill up with your most valuable cards.

Profile card

A card that's all yours

/profile shows your level, XP bar, streak, bio, card and set counts and your collection by tier in a single image. Its background comes from the cover of the first card in your showcase.

  • Add a quote of up to 100 characters with /bio.
  • Music taste: your 3 most-played artists appear on your profile. It's off by default; turn it on with /privacy music-taste.
  • You earn XP by chatting and listening; levelling up brings a short celebration card.
Your own listening

Your songs come out of the box

About 25% of box cards come from songs you played on this server and don't own yet, 50% from the server pool and 25% from other servers.

Your own share grows gradually until the songs you played reach 40 cards. You need to have joined with /start and have tracking on; /rates shows where your own share stands.

Be the first on your server to play a song and your name appears on its card as the discoverer.

/random

Your server's discoveries

Suggests a random song discovered on your server: song, artist, album, tier and who discovered it. Songs played by members who haven't joined the game appear anonymously.

Rare and higher songs come animated: a glow or stars in the tier colour.

Dismantling and crafting

Duplicates aren't wasted

Every box gives an extra 3 Shards no matter what's inside. If you get a card you already own, the duplicate turns into Shards automatically.

/card dismantle turns a card you don't want into Shards (Epic and above ask for confirmation). With /card craft you spend Shards to make a missing card yourself: from the pools of servers you're in or from songs you played. Crafting a Legendary needs a profile at least 14 days old.
