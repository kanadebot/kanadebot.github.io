🎮 Play

Start, open boxes, daily reward and quests.

  • /start

    Join the game, get your welcome gift and open your first box.

    With prefix: k!start

  • /box

    Open a music box (streak box, free box or 100 Vinyl).

    With prefix: k!box

  • /rates

    Show box rates, pity counters and your boxes.

    With prefix: k!rates

  • /daily

    Your daily Vinyl reward, today's quests and your streak.

    With prefix: k!daily

  • /vote

    Vote for Kanade on top.gg and claim your Vinyl reward.

    With prefix: k!vote

  • /help

    Show commands by category.

    With prefix: k!help

Cards & Collection

Show, inspect and craft cards; collection and sets.

  • /collection [member] [rarity] [page]

    Your card collection: filter by rarity, browse page by page.

    Options

    • member Whose collection to show (empty: yours)
    • rarity Only cards of this tier
    • page Page to open

    With prefix: k!collection [member] [rarity] [page]

  • /sets

    Your progress on album sets.

    With prefix: k!sets

  • /card show <card>

    Share one of your cards in the chat.

    Options

    • card The card to show

    With prefix: k!card show <card>

  • /card info <card>

    Edition, tier and set details of a card.

    Options

    • card The card to inspect

    With prefix: k!card info <card>

  • /card craft <card>

    Craft a card with Shards.

    Options

    • card The card to craft

    With prefix: k!card craft <card>

  • /card dismantle <card>

    Turn a card you don't want into Shards (the card is removed).

    Options

    • card Card to dismantle (name or artist)

    With prefix: k!card dismantle <card>

  • /card showcase [slot] <card>

    Puts one of your cards in the showcase on your profile card.

    Options

    • slot Showcase slot 1-3 (leave empty for the first free slot)
    • card Card to put in your showcase

    With prefix: k!card showcase [slot] <card>

🏆 Social

Server leaderboard, profile card and bio.

  • /leaderboard [period]

    Options

    With prefix: k!leaderboard [period]

  • /profile [member]

  • /bio <text>

    Options

    With prefix: k!bio <text>

⚙️ Settings & Privacy

Your data and tracking choice; server settings (requires Manage Server) and bot status.

  • /privacy status

    Shows the data kept about you and your tracking status.

    With prefix: k!privacy

  • /privacy opt-out

    Turns off tracking of your Spotify listening (on every server).

    With prefix: k!privacy opt-out

  • /privacy opt-in

    Turns Spotify listening tracking back on.

    With prefix: k!privacy opt-in

  • /privacy music-taste <state>

    Shows or hides your top 3 artists on your profile card.

    Options

    • state On or off

    With prefix: k!privacy music-taste <state>

  • /privacy delete-my-data

    Permanently deletes all of your game data (asks for confirmation).

    With prefix: k!privacy delete-my-data

  • /settings showRequires the Manage Server permission

    Show the current server settings.

    With prefix: k!settings

  • /settings language <language>Requires the Manage Server permission

    Change the bot's language on this server.

    Options

    • language New language

    With prefix: k!settings language <language>

  • /settings tracking <state>Requires the Manage Server permission

    Turn Spotify listening tracking on or off.

    Options

    • state On or off

    With prefix: k!settings tracking <state>

  • /settings announce-channel [channel]Requires the Manage Server permission

    Set the channel for announcements such as level-ups.

    Options

    • channel Announcement channel (empty: the channel where the message was sent)

    With prefix: k!settings announce-channel [channel]

  • /ping

    Show the bot's latency.

    With prefix: k!ping
