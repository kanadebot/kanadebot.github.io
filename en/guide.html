Guide: how to play

How to play and terms: Vinyl, Shards, tiers, sets, streaks, pity.

Step by step

  1. 1. Listen: every song you play on Spotify (at least 30 s) earns 3 Vinyl (up to 30 songs a day). Played songs join the server pool and become cards.
  2. 2. Open boxes: /box = 100 Vinyl. Tier rates are always in /rates. The card comes ~25% from songs you played on this server and don't own yet, ~50% from the server pool, ~25% from other servers.
  3. 3. Collect: duplicates turn into Shards; craft a missing card with /card craft. Turn a card you don't want into Shards with /card dismantle.
  4. 4. Every day: /daily reward + 3 quests; finish all three for 1 free box.

Terms

Vinyl

Game currency. Earned by listening (3/song), /daily (100+) and quests; opens boxes (100 Vinyl).

Shards

+3 from every box; duplicates give Common 5 · Rare 15 · Epic 50 · Legendary 150. Crafting costs 30 / 90 / 300 / 900.

Tier

Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary. A card always keeps the tier it was minted with.

#️⃣ Edition no.

Which copy of that song you own (#1 = first edition). Numbers are never reused.

Set

At least 3 songs of one album in the catalog. Collect them all for 30 Vinyl per song; /sets.

Streak

Claiming /daily on consecutive days. +10 Vinyl per day; every 7 days an Epic+ guaranteed box.

🎯 Pity (guarantee)

Bad-luck protection: Epic+ is certain within 10 boxes in a row, Legendary within 45.

Pool (own / server / global)

Where a box picks its card: ~25% own (songs you played on this server and don't own yet), ~50% server (songs played on this server), ~25% global (other servers). While your own pool is small its share goes to the server; while the server pool is small its share goes to global.

Why is Spotify needed?

The bot only sees Discord's "listening" status. If it's hidden you earn no Vinyl and the pool doesn't grow: Settings → Connections → Spotify → "Display as status".

Numbers may change for balance; current rates are in /rates.
